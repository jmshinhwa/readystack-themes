Free scope
  Maps every go.mod line that breaks go get for your users (module path, go line, toolchain, replace, require) and prints the exact replacement line. is free for any use, personal or commercial, for as long as you keep the
  extension installed, and the online check at the product page is free without registration.

Paid scope
  A dated release-gate report per tag with the go.dev rule cited for every line, plus a CI exit code that blocks the tag. is the paid part. It asks for a licence key issued at purchase (one seat, one key). The key is checked against the payment provider and
  cached locally for 30 days so it keeps working offline.
