pypdf Migration Check - PyMuPDF AGPL Licence Gate — Licence

Free scope
  Maps every PyMuPDF/fitz line in the open file (imports, calls, requirements, pyproject, environment.yml) to the exact pypdf, pypdfium2 or pdfplumber replacement line. is free for any use, personal or commercial, for as long as you keep the
  extension installed, and the online check at the product page is free without registration.

Paid scope
  Whole-workspace migration plan (each use mapped to its replacement), a dated AGPL exposure report for the company and a CI gate that fails builds adding new PyMuPDF use. is the paid part. It asks for a licence key issued at purchase (one seat, one key). The key is checked against the payment provider and
  cached locally for 30 days so it keeps working offline.
