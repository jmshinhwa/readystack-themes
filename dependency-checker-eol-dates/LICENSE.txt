Dependency Checker — EOL Dates — Licence

Free scope
  Checks the open manifest and lists every pinned framework major that is past or within 120 days of its end-of-support date, with the date and the upgrade target. is free for any use, personal or commercial, for as long as you keep the
  extension installed, and the online check at the product page is free without registration.

Paid scope
  Scans every package.json, composer.json and requirements file in the workspace at once and exports one dated EOL report (CSV + Markdown) for a client or audit. is the paid part. It asks for a licence key issued at purchase (one seat, one key). The key is checked against the payment provider and
  cached locally for 30 days so it keeps working offline.

Redistribution

  engine are provided as-is; check results are advisory and do not constitute legal advice.
