Free scope
  Flags every Anaconda paid-channel line in the open environment.yml or .condarc and shows the conda-forge replacement, no key needed. is free for any use, personal or commercial, for as long as you keep the
  extension installed, and the online check at the product page is free without registration.

Paid scope
  Scans every environment file and .condarc in the workspace at once and exports one licence-audit report you keep for procurement or security review. is the paid part. It asks for a licence key issued at purchase (one seat, one key). The key is checked against the payment provider and
  cached locally for 30 days so it keeps working offline.
