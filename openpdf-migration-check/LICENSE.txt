OpenPDF Migration Check: iText AGPL Gate — Licence

Free scope
  Maps every iText (AGPLv3) dependency in the open pom.xml or build.gradle to its line and the exact OpenPDF 3.0.5 or PDFBox 3.0.8 line that replaces it. is free for any use, personal or commercial, for as long as you keep the
  extension installed, and the online check at the product page is free without registration.

Paid scope
  Whole-workspace migration plan: every module and every iText artifact mapped to its replacement in a dated report, plus a CI gate that fails a build adding new com.itextpdf use. is the paid part. It asks for a licence key issued at purchase (one seat, one key). The key is checked against the payment provider and
  cached locally for 30 days so it keeps working offline.
