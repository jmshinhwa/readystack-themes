# Privacy Policy for Kids — DinoDash

Last updated: March 3, 2024

DinoDash cares about your family's privacy. This policy explains how we treat information from
children under 16 who play DinoDash.

## Contact

Questions? E-mail us at support@dinodash.example and we will get back to you.

## Information we collect

We collect a nickname, an avatar choice and gameplay statistics. We use cookies and analytics to
understand how the game is played and to improve it.

## Sharing

We may share information with third parties, including our partners and service providers, in order
to operate and improve DinoDash.

## Data retention

We retain your child's information indefinitely so that progress is never lost.

## Your choices

Parents can contact us to review or delete their child's account at any time.

## Schools

Teachers sometimes use DinoDash in the classroom as a reading warm-up.

## Legal

The compliance deadline for the amended COPPA Rule is June 23, 2025, and DinoDash meets it.
