# Cyber Incident Response Plan

Last reviewed: 2024-11-05
Owner: Head of IT

## Purpose

This plan describes how we respond to cyber incidents and how we meet our reporting duties under the NIS2 Directive.

## Severity levels

- P1: production down for customers
- P2: degraded service
- P3: internal only

## Reporting steps

1. The incident commander declares a P1 and opens a bridge.
2. Send an early warning to the authorities within 48 hours of the incident being contained.
3. Submit the incident notification to the regulator within 5 days, with an initial assessment of the
   incident, its severity and its business impact.
4. Submit the final report within 14 days of the notification, describing what happened and the root cause.
