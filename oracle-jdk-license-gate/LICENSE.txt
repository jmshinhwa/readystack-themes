Oracle JDK License Gate — Licence

Free scope
  Names every Oracle-licensed Java runtime in the file you have open, with its license basis, its free-window status on today's date and the drop-in OpenJDK line that replaces it. is free for any use, personal or commercial, for as long as you keep the
  extension installed, and the online check at the product page is free without registration.

Paid scope
  Sweeps every file in the workspace and writes a dated evidence pack - file, line, distribution, license basis and swap - to hand to procurement or an Oracle audit, plus team and commercial use. is the paid part. It asks for a licence key issued at purchase (one seat, one key). The key is checked against the payment provider and
  cached locally for 30 days so it keeps working offline.
