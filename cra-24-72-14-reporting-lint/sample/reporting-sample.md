# Security

We take security seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability
Please use the form on our website. We will acknowledge your report within 5 business days.
We aim to ship a fix before public disclosure.

## Regulatory
Under the EU Cyber Resilience Act our reporting obligations start in December 2027, so
nothing changes for us yet. When they do apply we will notify the supervisory authority.

Because the GDPR already gives us 72 hours for a personal data breach, we will reuse
the same runbook and the same contacts.

## Support
This release is supported until 2026-06-30. After that, upgrade to the current major
version to keep receiving security updates until 2027-03-31.
