Webfont License Audit — Licence

Free scope
  Scans every CSS, SCSS and LESS file in the workspace and lists each webfont licence risk with its rule id, severity and line number. is free for any use, personal or commercial, for as long as you keep the
  extension installed, and the online check at the product page is free without registration.

Paid scope
  Exports the whole finding list as a dated FONT-LICENSE-AUDIT.md plus CSV evidence pack you keep and hand to a foundry auditor or your own legal team. is the paid part. It asks for a licence key issued at purchase (one seat, one key;
  7-day full refund, no questions). The key is checked against the payment provider and
  cached locally for 30 days so it keeps working offline.

Redistribution
  You may not resell, re-host or bundle this extension or its rule set. The rule set and the
  engine are provided as-is; check results are advisory and do not constitute legal advice.
