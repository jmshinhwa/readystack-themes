Free scope
  Every finding and the per-container list-price exposure, in the editor and in the web page, free with no key is free for any use, personal or commercial, for as long as you keep the
  extension installed, and the online check at the product page is free without registration.

Paid scope
  Workspace-wide licence inventory: one CSV/Markdown table of every SQL Server container, its edition, its core count and its list-price cost, ready to hand to a licence auditor is the paid part. It asks for a licence key issued at purchase (one seat, one key). The key is checked against the payment provider and
  cached locally for 30 days so it keeps working offline.
