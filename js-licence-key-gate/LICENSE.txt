JS Licence Key Gate: GPL & Eval Keys — Licence

Free scope
  Check one open JS/TS file for GPL, evaluation and missing commercial licence keys, with the line and the fix is free for any use, personal or commercial, for as long as you keep the
  extension installed, and the online check at the product page is free without registration.

Paid scope
  Scan every file in the workspace in one pass and export the licence inventory (CSV + Markdown) for procurement or due diligence is the paid part. It asks for a licence key issued at purchase (one seat, one key). The key is checked against the payment provider and
  cached locally for 30 days so it keeps working offline.

Redistribution
  You may not resell, re-host or bundle this extension or its rule set. The rule set and the
  engine are provided as-is; check results are advisory and do not constitute legal advice.
