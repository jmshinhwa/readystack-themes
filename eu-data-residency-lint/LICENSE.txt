EU Data Residency Lint (Terraform, GDPR Ch. V) — Licence

Free scope
  Scan any Terraform, Bicep or YAML file and see every cloud region that leaves the EEA, with the country and the transfer basis it would need is free for any use, personal or commercial, for as long as you keep the
  extension installed, and the online check at the product page is free without registration.

Paid scope
  Sweep the whole repository and keep a dated Chapter V transfer register in Markdown — every file, line, region, country and adequacy status in one auditable document is the paid part. It asks for a licence key issued at purchase (one seat, one key). The key is checked against the payment provider and
  cached locally for 30 days so it keeps working offline.
