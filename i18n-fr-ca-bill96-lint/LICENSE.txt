i18n fr-CA Lint — Quebec Bill 96 — Licence

Free scope
  Lints the fr-CA locale file you have open and lists every line an OQLF complaint would land on, with the Quebec wording to use instead. is free for any use, personal or commercial, for as long as you keep the
  extension installed, and the online check at the product page is free without registration.

Paid scope
  Scans every locale file in the workspace in one pass, exports the findings as CSV or JSON you keep, and returns a CI exit code. is the paid part. It asks for a licence key issued at purchase (one seat, one key). The key is checked against the payment provider and
  cached locally for 30 days so it keeps working offline.

Redistribution
  You may not resell, re-host or bundle this extension or its rule set. The rule set and the
  engine are provided as-is; check results are advisory and do not constitute legal advice.
