pubspec Publish Gate - pub.dev pre-publish check — Licence

Free scope
  Maps every pubspec.yaml line that pub.dev will refuse or silently ignore, and prints the exact replacement line for each one - no key needed. is free for any use, personal or commercial, for as long as you keep the
  extension installed, and the online check at the product page is free without registration.

Paid scope
  Dated pre-publish report for every pubspec.yaml in a workspace, plus a CI exit-code gate that stops a bad version before it becomes permanent on pub.dev. is the paid part. It asks for a licence key issued at purchase (one seat, one key). The key is checked against the payment provider and
  cached locally for 30 days so it keeps working offline.
