Pekko Migration Gate — Akka BSL licence check — Licence

Free scope
  Checks the open build or config file: every Akka jar still under BSL 1.1 on your date, every akka.* key, akka:// seed and old port Pekko ignores, with the Pekko line to paste. is free for any use, personal or commercial, for as long as you keep the
  extension installed, and the online check at the product page is free without registration.

Paid scope
  Workspace sweep: every build.sbt, pom.xml, build.gradle and .conf in the repo in one Markdown migration report for your licence owner. is the paid part. It asks for a licence key issued at purchase (one seat, one key). The key is checked against the payment provider and
  cached locally for 30 days so it keeps working offline.

Redistribution

  engine are provided as-is; check results are advisory and do not constitute legal advice.
