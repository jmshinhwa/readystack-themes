GDPR Privacy Notice Lint — Licence

Free scope
  Checking the privacy notice open in your editor against all 16 Article 13/14 checks is free for any use, personal or commercial, for as long as you keep the
  extension installed, and the online check at the product page is free without registration.

Paid scope
  Sweeping every notice in the workspace at once and writing a dated evidence report you keep is the paid part. It asks for a licence key issued at purchase (one seat, one key;
