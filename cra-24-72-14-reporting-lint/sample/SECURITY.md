# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please email security@example.com with a description of the issue and steps to reproduce.
We will acknowledge your report within 5 business days.

## Our obligations under the Cyber Resilience Act

If a vulnerability is actively exploited, we send an early warning to ENISA and our coordinating CSIRT within 24 hours of becoming aware of it.
As with GDPR Article 33, we notify within 72 hours.
Reporting obligations under Article 14 apply from 11 December 2027.
A final report follows within 14 days of a corrective measure being available.
If a severe incident affects the product, we report it to the European Commission.

## Supported versions

Version 3.x receives security updates until 2028-06-30.
