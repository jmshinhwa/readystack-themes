# Risk Assessment and Resilience Plan — Northgate Water Utility

Designation notified by the competent authority: 2026-08-14
First risk assessment completion target: 2027-06-30

## Hazard register

- Natural hazards: river flood, storm damage, summer drought and heatwave stress on reservoirs.
- Accidents: chlorine dosing equipment failure, industrial spill upstream of the intake.
- Antagonistic threats: sabotage of pumping stations, terrorism, insider manipulation of SCADA set points.

## Dependencies

The service depends on grid electricity (energy sector) and chemical deliveries; two hospitals depend on our supply (cross-sector).

## Resilience measures

- Physical protection: perimeter fencing and access control at all treatment works.
- Business continuity: mobile generators and an alternative supply contract for chlorine.
- Personnel security: background checks for control-room staff and quarterly access rights review.
- Training: annual flood exercise and staff awareness sessions.

## Incident notification

1. Initial notification to the competent authority within 72 hours of detection.
2. Detailed incident report within 60 days.

## Review

The risk assessment is reviewed every five years.
