Free scope
  Check one product notice in the open file against all 13 Article 3(2)-(3) items and 4 quality rules, with line numbers and the fix for each gap. is free for any use, personal or commercial, for as long as you keep the
  extension installed, and the online check at the product page is free without registration.

Paid scope
  Check a whole folder of product notices at once and export a dated Data Act evidence report (.csv + .md) per SKU for your files. is the paid part. It asks for a licence key issued at purchase (one seat, one key). The key is checked against the payment provider and
  cached locally for 30 days so it keeps working offline.
