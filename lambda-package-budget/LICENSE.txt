Lambda Package Size Budget - 250 MB check — Licence

Free scope
  Ranks every listed dependency by measured unzipped MB against the 250 MB Lambda limit and prints the exact line to drop or swap. is free for any use, personal or commercial, for as long as you keep the
  extension installed, and the online check at the product page is free without registration.

Paid scope
  Dated size-budget report for the pull request, a CI gate that fails the build above your MB budget, and team licence keys. is the paid part. It asks for a licence key issued at purchase (one seat, one key). The key is checked against the payment provider and
  cached locally for 30 days so it keeps working offline.
